Bilbo privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Bilbo is a private, single-user application. Its only user is its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, Bilbo accesses the owner's Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) through Google APIs.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide features to the owner, such as summarising emails, alerting the owner to new messages and managing the owner's calendar. Data is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than serving the owner.

Bilbo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
